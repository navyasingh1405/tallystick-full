Four things that are normally separate projects, as byproducts of scoring one applicant.

  • 1 Every decision reproducible and tamper-evident
  • 2 Every decline with regulator-ready reasons
  • 3 Fairness testing and alternative search as code
  • 4 A validation document that writes itself
About this system A demonstration built on public FFIEC HMDA data. It holds no customer records and connects to nothing. Accounts exist so that actions in the audit trail have a name against them.
